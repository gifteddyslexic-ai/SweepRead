SweepReader Card Test

All finished cards

Tap an underlined word to test its card. The card opens with the word and picture, the voice says the word, any movement plays, and the definition sweeps in with the voice. Tap again to close. Movement cards follow the new picture rules; "on", "over", "under", "here" and "there" are still pictures with the red arrow, and "in" now uses the 3D box.

Words: on, off, in, out, up, down, into, onto, over, under, through, here, there, to, from, and new: back (three cards) and front (two cards).
