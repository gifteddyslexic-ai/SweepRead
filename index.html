Second Card Demo

Second card sliding out

Tap an underlined word. The card opens with the word and picture; the definition follows by itself as soon as the word has been read. Both words "left" have a second meaning: its card slides out from behind the first one straight away, tilted like a spread deck. The definition sweeps in from left to right as the voice says it, and the ball moves along its path with the dashed trail drawn in behind it; the definition waits until the ball has arrived. Tap again to bring it forward; tap once more to close. "into" has no second card, so the next tap closes it. The pictures and definitions for "left" are placeholders for Axel to replace. The box and the tube are drawn in 3D, so the ball passes behind their front parts.

  1. Tap: the card opens with the word and picture; the voice says the word 0.3 s later. As soon as the word has been read, the definition appears and is spoken.
  2. If there is a second meaning, its card slides out from behind, slightly tilted, as soon as the first card is showing.
  3. Next tap: the second card comes forward and runs the same way; with no second card, the card closes.
  4. A tap before the definition has appeared shows it straight away.
