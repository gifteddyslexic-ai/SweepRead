SweepReader Demo

Demo for Axel. Tap the text to sweep: in a sentence that hasn't started, any tap starts it from the beginning; in the sentence being read, tap in front of a word to read the sentence again from the start, or after a word to carry on from the next word. Tap an underlined word for its card: the word and picture, any movement, then the definition with the voice. Tap again for the next card (where there is one) or to close it and carry on reading. ☰ (top right) holds the settings, including Voice, trigger-word support and the on-screen button. On a keyboard: Space sweeps (tap, or hold to sweep while held), ← start of sentence, ↓ next word, ↑ card for the word under the sweep.
