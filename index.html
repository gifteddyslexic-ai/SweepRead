Flooent Reader Demo

Demo for Axel. Tap the button to sweep to the end of the sentence; tap again to pause. Press and hold to sweep only while you hold. Tap an underlined word for its pop-up, then tap the word or the pop-up again: 2 hear the word, 3 definition, 4 hear the definition, 5 close and carry on reading. Tap in front of a word (or the space before it) to read its sentence from the start; tap after a word (or the space after it) in the sentence being read to carry on from the next word. In a sentence that hasn't started, any tap starts it from the beginning. ☰ (top right) holds the settings. On a keyboard: Space = the button (tap, or hold to sweep while held), ← start of sentence, ↓ next word, ↑ pop-up for the word under the sweep.
